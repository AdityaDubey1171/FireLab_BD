01

What is a Firewall?

A firewall is a security system that monitors network traffic and decides whether traffic should be allowed or blocked according to predefined security rules.

What it is

A security control placed between a protected system and the traffic that tries to reach it.

How it works

Each request is inspected and compared with predefined security rules.

What the firewall does

Allows traffic that is normal and blocks traffic that matches a threat rule.

Why it matters

It stops harmful requests before they can reach the application.

02

How Does a Firewall Work?

The firewall examines incoming or outgoing traffic and compares it against security rules. Select a step to learn more.

What it is

The traffic that wants to reach the protected system.

How it works

The request is sent towards the application and meets the firewall first.

What the firewall does

Stops the request at the boundary for inspection.

Why it matters

Every request passes the same checkpoint.

What it is

Reading the source, target, protocol and content of the request.

How it works

The firewall collects the facts it needs to make a decision.

What the firewall does

Prepares the data for rule matching.

Why it matters

Accurate information leads to accurate decisions.

What it is

Matching the request against the defined rule set.

How it works

Each rule describes a known threat pattern and an action.

What the firewall does

Looks for a rule that fits the request.

Why it matters

Rules are the policy that guides every decision.

What it is

Deciding whether the request is a known threat or normal traffic.

How it works

A matching rule gives the category and severity of the threat.

What the firewall does

Labels the request so the right action can be chosen.

Why it matters

Severity helps administrators decide what to review first.

What it is

The final decision for the request.

How it works

Threats are blocked. Normal traffic is allowed to continue.

What the firewall does

Rejects harmful requests before they reach the system.

Why it matters

This step is what actually protects the application.

What it is

A saved record of the decision.

How it works

The event is stored so it can be reviewed later.

What the firewall does

Keeps a history of what happened.

Why it matters

Logs support investigation and reporting.
